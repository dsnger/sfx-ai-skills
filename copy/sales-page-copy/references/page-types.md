# Page types

## Main page (offer with packages)

Header (claim with a result word, subline with scope, CTA, seals, sticker) → three pain questions → solution (hand over three worries, CTA) → strongest differentiator as a demo → implementation per pain (product views) → trust/industry (why we can do this) → quality (review report) → contract (costs, rights, contact person) → process (steps with graphics, CTA) → prices (rent/buy switch, slider, recommendation, calculation example per package, comparison collapsible) → FAQ (7) → contact (conversation, small hint at the alternative offer).

## Package page (detail page)

The reader is already interested. They need emotional confirmation, then the main page's arguments backed up, only then details.

1. Hero: package name, who it's for, price large, three highlights, CTA, mood photo with sticker.
2. "Darauf können Sie sich freuen": five experience sentences that hold for every reader.
3. "Das steckt in jedem Paket": five core promises as a row list.
4. Features as an accordion per area.
5. Rent or buy; calculation example open ("Wann rechnet sich …?").
6. All-round service (short, five-point list, small product view).
7. Process with large numbers; checkpoints (results that solve real pains).
8. FAQ with seven questions: general plus package-specific.
9. Fork "Passt ein anderes Paket besser?", contact.

## Presentation

Same reader chain as the main page, condensed: one statement per slide, a visible transition between slides, speaker notes follow `copy-rules` as well. Inform first, then offer; readers should recognize their own gaps without fear-mongering or sales pressure.

## Pricing and offer logic (proven)

- Describe packages by building blocks, not by number of pages; put everything essential for being found already in the smallest package.
- Calculation examples only with real, collected averages (source, date), recalculated by script; "entspricht dem Jahresbetrag", not "trägt das Paket".
- Rent as rent-to-own with all-round service afterwards; "ohne Anfangsinvestition statt [Kaufpreis]"; purchase in two installments (draft approval / completion).
- Calculation basis: hourly rate, list prices of own products, hosting, AI costs; quotas as yearly averages.
- No guarantee of success; an honest FAQ answer plus a review after six months.
