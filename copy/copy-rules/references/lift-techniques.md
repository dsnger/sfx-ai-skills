# Lift techniques

Ways a professional copywriter lifts a plain, correct draft into copy that sells. Use them when writing new copy and when reworking a draft. They work inside the rules of SKILL.md, not against them: each one is an option to check, not a duty for every line. Apply a technique where it makes the line stronger, and leave it where the line is already strong.

1. **End each point with what it means for the reader.** A service line names the consequence the reader cares about.
   - Before: "Wir prüfen Ihre Abrechnungen sorgfältig."
   - After: "Wir prüfen Ihre Abrechnungen sorgfältig. Nur so stimmen auch Ihre Kennzahlen."

2. **Name the reader's situation, then the relief.** First a plain fact about the reader's life that holds for nearly every reader, then what the offer takes off their plate. Never an invented scene or an assumed feeling.
   - "Im Tagesgeschäft bleibt wenig Zeit, die Zahlen ständig im Blick zu behalten. Das nehmen wir Ihnen ab."

3. **Offer a benefit the reader did not think of.** One concrete extra the offer brings beyond the obvious task. It can come from the facts and the briefing, or from general knowledge of the trade: what a service like this typically makes possible. Use it with confidence; where the business itself hasn't confirmed it, add a question to the client.
   - "Sprechen Sie uns an, bevor Sie Gehälter erhöhen. Oft gibt es Spielräume, die beiden Seiten nutzen."

4. **Ask the reader's own questions.** Put the question the reader already has in their head, then answer it. It must be a real question with a real answer, not an empty yes-question.
   - "Welche Daten dürfen Sie speichern, welche nicht? Wir schaffen Klarheit."

5. **Say for whom it matters most.** When the readers differ, name the groups and say where the offer helps most, instead of one line for everybody.
   - "Bei mehreren Standorten kommen mehr Anforderungen zusammen. Hier lohnt sich spezialisierte Beratung besonders."

6. **Give a claim its reason.** A statement about quality is followed by why it is true.
   - "Ihr Ansprechpartner kennt Ihre Abläufe. Denn er betreut seit Jahren Betriebe wie Ihren."

7. **Name the roles, not just "Ansprechpartner".** Who exactly does the work makes the service tangible.
   - Before: "feste Ansprechpartner für Ihre Buchhaltung"
   - After: "Erfahrene Buchhalter kümmern sich um Ihre Buchhaltung."

8. **Buttons with a concrete offer.** The button says what the reader gets by clicking, not that the sender is happy.
   - Before: "Wir freuen uns auf ein Gespräch."
   - After: "Kostenloses Erstgespräch vereinbaren"

*Derived from comparing a generated draft with its revision by a professional copywriter.*
