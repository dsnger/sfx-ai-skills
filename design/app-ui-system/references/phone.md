# Phone

- **Like a native app:** clear buttons, not too full, important things in reach. The daily work as comfortable as possible, managing as reduced as possible. The app must work perfectly at phone width.
- **Check every page and flow** at the phone widths the profile lists: no sideways scroll, nothing outside, nothing squeezed.
- **Narrow fallbacks are global,** set once (for example: below a certain width buttons drop their icon, the search shows a shorter placeholder), never per screen.
- **Filters open in a sheet** with full-width controls and words.
